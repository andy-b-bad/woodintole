Birchwood Court, Rotherham

South Yorkshire 3 bedrooms Acquired March 2023 Occupied · Fully let

Overview

A well-located terraced house with strong rental demand

Birchwood Court is a three-bedroom terraced house in a well-established residential area of Rotherham, close to local transport links and amenities. The property forms part of our completed portfolio of acquisitions let to social housing providers.

Property type: terraced house. Bedrooms: three. Current use: social housing letting.

Why this opportunity was selected

Ownership and condition signals pointed to an off-market opportunity

Our sourcing process identified the property through limited company ownership research. The owning company had held the property for close to a decade, and available condition indicators suggested it had not been recently modernised — both signals consistent with a motivated seller and refurbishment potential.

Acquisition story

From identification to exchange

Following verification by our acquisitions team, a direct approach was made to the registered company. Terms were agreed within six weeks, and the acquisition completed shortly afterwards without the property having been publicly marketed.

Original condition

Dated but structurally sound

The property was in lettable but dated condition, with an ageing kitchen and bathroom, older electrical wiring, and general wear consistent with a long-term single tenancy.

Refurbishment and improvement work

Brought up to housing provider standard

Refurbishment work included a new kitchen and bathroom, rewiring throughout, redecoration, new flooring and external repairs, completed to meet the specification required by our social housing partner.

Social housing use & occupancy

Let on a long-term agreement

The property is let in full to a registered social housing provider on a multi-year agreement, with the provider responsible for tenant placement and day-to-day housing management.

Occupancy status: fully occupied. Housing provider status: active, long-term agreement in place.
