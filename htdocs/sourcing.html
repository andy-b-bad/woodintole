AI-assisted property sourcing

Finding the opportunities that manual research misses.

Our sourcing process brings together UK property, company ownership and civic data, then applies AI-assisted analysis to highlight acquisitions worth attention — particularly properties owned by limited companies, where ownership and intent are often unclear from public listings alone.

The process

A seven-stage process from data to decision.

Each stage narrows a wide dataset down to a small number of acquisitions worth serious attention.

01 · Requirements

The first filter is you

What are you looking for and where. The first filter is you.

02 · Instant discovery

Surface candidate properties

Multitude candidate properties are surfaced against a range of overlapping attributes.

03 · Analyse/ refine

Assess interest and progress

Condition, risk and investment indicators combine to assess interest and progress.

04 · Outreach

Bespoke comms at scale

A single button press = instant, bulk creation of comms and CRM entry. Every letter is bespoke to its own deal.

05 · Response/ escalation

Score replies before negotiation

Reply data is further scored via an automated valuation pipeline before negotiation begins.

06 · Negotiate

Anchor discussions in data

Discussions anchored in proven data facillitate realistic, beneficial outcomes for both parties.

07 · Introduce or acquire

A decision, not just a report

Verified opportunities are introduced to investors or progressed to acquisition, refurbishment and letting — as shown in our completed portfolio.

Who this helps

Built for the people who act on property information.

Property investors

Fewer, better-qualified leads

Spend time evaluating opportunities that have already passed ownership, condition and yield screening, rather than sifting listings by hand.

Developers

Sites and stock with context

Planning history, ownership structure and local demand data assembled alongside each opportunity, rather than gathered separately after interest is confirmed.

Property acquisition businesses

A research layer that scales

Extend your own acquisition pipeline with a consistent, repeatable research process rather than variable manual effort.

Social housing partners

Suitable stock, assessed early

Properties are considered against social housing suitability from the outset, not retrofitted to the requirement later.

Organisations seeking off-market opportunities

Access to properties before they are widely marketed

Ownership-led research surfaces opportunities connected to owners who have not listed a property publicly, widening the pool beyond agent-marketed stock.
